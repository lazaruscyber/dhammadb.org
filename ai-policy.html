AI Policy

Some articles on DhammaDB are written with the help of artificial intelligence. This page explains what the label above means, how we use AI, and how we keep our articles faithful to the Theravāda tradition.

What the label means

An article marked Contains AI-generated content includes text that was first drafted, summarised or translated with an AI tool. The label does not mean the article is unreviewed or unreliable. It tells you how the text was produced so you can read it with that in mind.

How we use AI

  • Drafting and structuring articles from source material chosen by our editors.
  • Summarising longer passages and producing first drafts of translations.
  • Checking spelling, consistency of Pāli terms and formatting.

What we do not use AI for

  • Deciding matters of doctrine. Our articles follow the orthodox Theravāda position as found in the Pāli Tipiṭaka and its commentaries, not the opinions of an AI system.
  • Inventing quotations, references or citations. Every reference should point to a real source that can be checked.
  • Replacing the canonical texts. Where an article and the Tipiṭaka differ, the Tipiṭaka is authoritative.

Review by people

AI tools can make mistakes, including confident ones. Content produced with AI is checked by our editors against the original sources before it is published, and articles are corrected as errors are found.

Reporting a mistake

If you find an error in any article, please tell us. Open a ticket on our Discord server with the article's Page ID, found at the bottom of every page, as described on the Contribute page.
