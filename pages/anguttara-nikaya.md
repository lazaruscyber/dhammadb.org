The **Aṅguttara Nikāya** ({{lang|Pāli|Aṅguttara Nikāya}}), "the Numerical Discourses", is the fourth of the five collections of the [[Sutta Piṭaka]]. Its discourses are arranged in eleven books ({{term|nipāta}}) according to the number of items they discuss, from the Book of Ones to the Book of Elevens. The commentaries count 9,557 suttas in it.[^dha] According to the commentaries it was entrusted after the [[First Council]] to the Venerable [[Anuruddha]] and his pupils.[^dha]

## Contents

The Book of Ones contains the chapter on the foremost disciples ({{term|etadagga}}), in which the Blessed One names the bhikkhus, nuns, laymen and laywomen who were foremost in particular qualities. Other well-known discourses include the [[Kālāma Sutta]] (AN 3.65), the discourse on the three kinds of [[Uposatha]] (AN 3.70), the five subjects for frequent recollection (AN 5.57) and the [[Girimānanda Sutta]] (AN 10.60).

Because it treats so many matters of daily life, the Aṅguttara Nikāya contains much of the Blessed One's teaching to lay followers.

[^dha]: *Sumaṅgalavilāsinī*, the commentary to the Dīgha Nikāya, introduction (*nidānakathā*).
