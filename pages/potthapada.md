**Poṭṭhapāda** ({{lang|Pāli|Poṭṭhapāda}}) is the sixth month of the Theravāda lunar calendar in the traditional Pāli listing, falling in about August to September in the Gregorian calendar. Like every month of the calendar, it takes its name from the constellation ({{term|nakkhatta}}) near which the moon is full in that month.

## In the calendar

Poṭṭhapāda falls in the middle of the rainy season ({{term|vassāna}}), while the monks keep the rains residence. It has 30 days, follows [[Sāvaṇa]] and is followed by [[Assayuja]]. Like every month, it contains four uposatha observance days: the eighth day of the waxing moon, the full moon, the eighth day of the waning moon and the new moon.

## Observances

In Sri Lanka the full moon of this month is observed as Binara Poya, which tradition associates with the founding of the order of nuns ({{term|bhikkhunī saṅgha}}) through the going forth of Mahāpajāpatī Gotamī.[^sl]
