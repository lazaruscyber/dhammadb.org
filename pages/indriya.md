**Indriya** ({{lang|Pāli|indriya}}) means faculty, a controlling power. The five spiritual faculties are the fourth group of the thirty-seven requisites of awakening; when unshakeable they are called the five powers ({{term|bala}}), the fifth group.[^sn48]

## The five faculties

| Faculty | Pāli | Seen in |
|---|---|---|
| Faith | {{term|saddhā}} | The four factors of stream-entry |
| Energy | {{term|viriya}} | The four right strivings |
| Mindfulness | {{term|sati}} | The four foundations of mindfulness |
| Concentration | {{term|samādhi}} | The four jhānas |
| Wisdom | {{term|paññā}} | The Four Noble Truths |

## Balancing the faculties

The [[Visuddhimagga]] teaches that faith must be balanced with wisdom, and energy with concentration. Too much faith with little wisdom leads to credulity; too much wisdom with little faith leads to cunning. Too much energy leads to restlessness; too much concentration leads to sloth. Mindfulness is always useful, like salt in every curry.[^vism]

## Other faculties

The [[Vibhaṅga]] lists twenty-two faculties, including the six sense faculties, the femininity and masculinity faculties, the life faculty, the five feeling faculties, the five spiritual faculties and three supramundane faculties.[^vbh]
