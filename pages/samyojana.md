The **saṃyojanas** ({{lang|Pāli|saṃyojana}}) are the ten fetters which bind beings to the round of rebirth. The four stages of the noble path are defined by which fetters they cut off.[^an1013]

## The ten fetters

| Fetter | Pāli | Abandoned by |
|---|---|---|
| Personality view | {{term|sakkāyadiṭṭhi}} | Stream-entry |
| Doubt | {{term|vicikicchā}} | Stream-entry |
| Clinging to rules and observances | {{term|sīlabbataparāmāsa}} | Stream-entry |
| Sensual desire | {{term|kāmarāga}} | Weakened by once-return, abandoned by non-return |
| Ill will | {{term|byāpāda}} | Weakened by once-return, abandoned by non-return |
| Desire for fine-material existence | {{term|rūparāga}} | Arahantship |
| Desire for immaterial existence | {{term|arūparāga}} | Arahantship |
| Conceit | {{term|māna}} | Arahantship |
| Restlessness | {{term|uddhacca}} | Arahantship |
| Ignorance | {{term|avijjā}} | Arahantship |

The first five are the lower fetters ({{term|orambhāgiya}}), binding beings to the sense sphere; the last five are the higher fetters ({{term|uddhambhāgiya}}).[^an1013]

## The noble persons

See [[Sotāpanna]], [[Sakadāgāmī]], [[Anāgāmī]] and [[Arahant]]. In the [[Abhidhamma Piṭaka|Abhidhamma]] a slightly different list of ten fetters is also given, including envy and avarice.[^dhs]

[^an1013]: Aṅguttara Nikāya 10.13, *Saṃyojana Sutta*.
[^dhs]: *Dhammasaṅgaṇī*, Saṃyojanagocchaka.
