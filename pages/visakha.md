**Visākhā** ({{lang|Pāli|Visākhā}}) was the chief female lay supporter of the Blessed One, declared foremost among the female lay followers who are givers.[^an1]

## Early life

According to the commentary she attained stream-entry at the age of seven when the Blessed One visited her town of Bhaddiya. Her family later moved to Sāketa. She married Puṇṇavaddhana, son of Migāra, a treasurer of [[Sāvatthī]] who followed the naked ascetics. Through her patience and wisdom she brought her father-in-law to the Dhamma; he attained stream-entry and called her his mother, so she became known as Migāramātā, "Migāra's mother".[^dhpa]

## The eight boons

The Vinaya records that she asked the Blessed One for eight boons: to give for life bathing cloths for the bhikkhus during the rains, food for incoming bhikkhus, food for departing bhikkhus, food for the sick, food for those nursing the sick, medicine for the sick, a regular supply of rice gruel, and bathing cloths for the nuns. Asked her reason, she explained the benefit of each, and the Blessed One granted them.[^vin]

## The Eastern Park

She sold her great jewelled ornament, the {{term|mahālatāpasādhana}}, and with the money built the Eastern Park ({{term|Pubbārāma}}) with its two-storeyed mansion, Migāramātupāsāda, where the Blessed One often stayed. The [[Aggañña Sutta]] and the [[Ānāpānassati Sutta]] were taught there.[^dhpa]
