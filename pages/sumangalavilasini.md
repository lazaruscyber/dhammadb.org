The **Sumaṅgalavilāsinī** ({{lang|Pāli|Sumaṅgalavilāsinī}}), "the Auspicious Joy", is the commentary to the [[Dīgha Nikāya]], compiled in Pāli by the Venerable [[Buddhaghosa]].

Its introduction gives an account of the [[First Council]] and of the classification of the Buddha's word: into the three Piṭakas, the five Nikāyas, the nine factors and the 84,000 units of the Dhamma. It records the count of the discourses in each collection and tells to which group of reciters each Nikāya was entrusted.
