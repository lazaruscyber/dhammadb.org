The **vipassanā ñāṇas** ({{lang|Pāli|vipassanā ñāṇa}}) are the knowledges of insight: the successive stages of understanding through which the meditator passes in developing [[Vipassanā|insight]]. They are described in the [[Paṭisambhidāmagga]], the [[Visuddhimagga]] and the [[Abhidhammattha Saṅgaha]].[^vism]

## The stages

In the tradition of Myanmar, following the Visuddhimagga, the stages are commonly counted as sixteen:

:::columns 2
1. Analysing mentality-materiality
2. Discerning conditions
3. Comprehension
4. Rise and fall
5. Dissolution
6. Fearfulness
7. Danger
8. Disenchantment
9. Desire for deliverance
10. Reflection
11. Equanimity about formations
12. Conformity
13. Change of lineage
14. Path
15. Fruition
16. Reviewing
:::

The nine knowledges from rise and fall to conformity constitute the purification by knowledge and vision of the way ([[Satta visuddhi|the sixth purification]]).[^abhs]

## Change of lineage

The knowledge of change of lineage ({{term|gotrabhū-ñāṇa}}) is the moment when the mind, for the first time, takes [[Nibbāna]] as its object. It passes from the lineage of the worldlings to that of the noble ones. It is followed immediately by the path and fruition knowledges, and then by reviewing, in which the meditator reviews the path, the fruit, Nibbāna, the defilements abandoned and those remaining.[^vism]

## The Mahāsi tradition

The [[Mahāsi Sayadaw]] described the stages as experienced in practice in the treatise *The Progress of Insight* ({{term|Visuddhiñāṇakathā}}), written in Pāli.

[^vism]: Buddhaghosa, *Visuddhimagga*. Chapter XX to XXII.
[^abhs]: Anuruddha, *Abhidhammattha Saṅgaha*. Chapter IX.
