The **Manorathapūraṇī** ({{lang|Pāli|Manorathapūraṇī}}), "Fulfiller of Wishes", is the commentary to the [[Aṅguttara Nikāya]], compiled in Pāli by the Venerable [[Buddhaghosa]].

Its commentary on the chapter of the foremost disciples ({{term|etadagga}}) in the Book of Ones is the main source for the lives of the great disciples. For each bhikkhu, nun, layman and laywoman named there, it tells of the aspiration made at the time of an earlier Buddha, the life in which they met the Blessed One, and how they came to be declared foremost.
