The **Cūḷavedalla Sutta** ({{lang|Pāli|Cūḷavedalla Sutta}}), "the Shorter Series of Questions and Answers", is the forty-fourth discourse of the [[Majjhima Nikāya]]. In it the lay follower Visākha questions the nun Dhammadinnā, his former wife, who had gone forth and become an arahant.[^mn]

## Topics

- **Personality** ({{term|sakkāya}}): the five aggregates subject to clinging, and the twenty kinds of personality view.
- **The path and the three trainings**: right speech, action and livelihood are included in virtue; right effort, mindfulness and concentration in concentration; right view and intention in wisdom.
- **Formations**: bodily, verbal and mental.
- **The attainment of cessation** ({{term|nirodha-samāpatti}}).
- **Feelings** and the latent tendencies that underlie them.

When Visākha reported the conversation to the Blessed One, the Blessed One said that Dhammadinnā was wise, and that had Visākha asked the Blessed One, the answer would have been the same.[^mn] The Blessed One declared Dhammadinnā foremost among the nuns who teach the Dhamma.[^an1]

[^mn]: Majjhima Nikāya 44, *Cūḷavedalla Sutta*.
[^an1]: Aṅguttara Nikāya 1, *Etadaggavagga* (the foremost disciples).
