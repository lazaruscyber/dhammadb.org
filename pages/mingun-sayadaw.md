The **Mingun Sayadaw**, the Venerable Vicittasārābhivaṃsa, was a Burmese bhikkhu who memorised the entire [[Tipiṭaka]]. In 1954 he became the first bhikkhu to pass the government's Tipiṭakadhara examination, and was given the title Tipiṭakadhara Dhammabhaṇḍāgārika, "bearer of the three Piṭakas, keeper of the treasury of the Dhamma".[^mbv]

## Life

He was born in 1911 in Myingyan district in Upper Burma, became a novice as a boy and studied at Mingun, near Sagaing, from which his title comes.

## The Sixth Council

At the [[Sixth Council]] in Yangon (1954 to 1956), the Mingun Sayadaw served as the reciter ({{term|vissajjaka}}), answering from memory the questions put by the [[Mahāsi Sayadaw]], as the Venerables [[Ānanda]] and [[Upāli]] had answered those of the Venerable [[Mahākassapa]] at the [[First Council]].[^mbv]

## The Great Chronicle of Buddhas

At the request of the government, the Sayadaw wrote the *Mahābuddhavaṃsa*, a vast work in Burmese on the lives of the Buddhas and especially of Gotama Buddha, drawing on the Pāli canon, commentaries and sub-commentaries. It has been translated into English as *The Great Chronicle of Buddhas*.[^mbv]

## Other work

The Sayadaw founded the Tipiṭaka Nikāya monasteries and supported the training of later generations of Tipiṭakadhara bhikkhus. The Sayadaw passed away in 1993.
