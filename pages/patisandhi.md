**Paṭisandhi** ({{lang|Pāli|paṭisandhi}}), "relinking", is the first moment of consciousness in a new existence. It links the new life to the previous one. It arises immediately after the death consciousness of the previous life, with no gap between them.[^abhs]

## What passes on

No soul or self passes from one life to the next. The [[Visuddhimagga]] gives similes: an echo, the light of a lamp, the impression of a seal, and a reflection in a mirror. In each case the result does not come from the cause as a substance moving over, yet it does not arise without the cause.[^vism] In the [[Milindapañha]], the Venerable Nāgasena compares it to lighting one lamp from another: the flame is neither the same nor different.

## The object at death

At the end of a life, the dying consciousness takes as its object one of three: the kamma that will produce rebirth, a sign of that kamma ({{term|kamma-nimitta}}), or a sign of the destination ({{term|gati-nimitta}}). The rebirth-linking of the next life takes that same object, and it becomes the object of the life-continuum ([[Bhavaṅga]]) throughout that life.[^abhs]

## Kinds

Nineteen types of consciousness can perform the function of rebirth-linking:[^abhs]

| Destination | Rebirth consciousness |
|---|---|
| The four states of loss | Unwholesome-resultant investigating consciousness |
| Humans and some devas of the sense sphere | Wholesome-resultant investigating consciousness (for those with defective faculties) and eight great resultants |
| Fine-material realms | Five fine-material resultants |
| Immaterial realms | Four immaterial resultants |

Non-percipient beings are reborn with matter only and no consciousness.

[^abhs]: Anuruddha, *Abhidhammattha Saṅgaha*. Chapter V.
[^vism]: Buddhaghosa, *Visuddhimagga*. Chapter XVII.
