The **Buddhavaṃsa** ({{lang|Pāli|Buddhavaṃsa}}), "Chronicle of the Buddhas", is the fourteenth book of the [[Khuddaka Nikāya]]. In verse it tells the lives of the twenty-four Buddhas who preceded the Buddha Gotama, beginning with the Buddha Dīpaṅkara, and then the life of Gotama Buddha.

The Blessed One related it at Kapilavatthu, on the jewelled walkway created in the sky, at the request of the Venerable [[Sāriputta]].[^bv]

## Sumedha's aspiration

The book's best-known passage tells how, countless aeons ago, the ascetic Sumedha lay down in the mud so that the Buddha Dīpaṅkara could walk across without soiling the feet, and made the aspiration to become a Buddha. Dīpaṅkara Buddha declared that Sumedha would one day become the Buddha Gotama. From that time the Bodhisatta began fulfilling the ten [[Pāramī|perfections]].[^bv]

For each Buddha the book records the city of birth, the parents, the tree under which awakening was attained, the chief disciples and the length of life.

[^bv]: *Buddhavaṃsa*.
