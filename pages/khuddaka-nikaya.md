The **Khuddaka Nikāya** ({{lang|Pāli|Khuddaka Nikāya}}), "the Minor Collection", is the fifth of the five collections of the [[Sutta Piṭaka]]. Unlike the other four it is a collection of separate books, in verse and prose, of many different kinds: short texts for recitation, inspired utterances, verses of the elders, birth stories, histories and analytical works. The commentaries list fifteen books.[^dha]

## The books

| No. | Book | Contents |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | [[Khuddakapāṭha]] | Nine short texts for recitation |
| 2 | [[Dhammapada]] | 423 verses of the Dhamma |
| 3 | [[Udāna]] | Inspired utterances |
| 4 | [[Itivuttaka]] | "Thus it was said" |
| 5 | [[Sutta Nipāta]] | A collection of discourses, mostly in verse |
| 6 | [[Vimānavatthu]] | Stories of celestial mansions |
| 7 | [[Petavatthu]] | Stories of departed beings |
| 8 | [[Theragāthā]] | Verses of the elder bhikkhus |
| 9 | [[Therīgāthā]] | Verses of the elder nuns |
| 10 | [[Jātaka]] | Birth stories of the Bodhisatta |
| 11 | [[Niddesa]] | Exposition of parts of the Sutta Nipāta |
| 12 | [[Paṭisambhidāmagga]] | The path of discrimination |
| 13 | [[Apadāna]] | Stories of past lives of disciples |
| 14 | [[Buddhavaṃsa]] | History of the Buddhas |
| 15 | [[Cariyāpiṭaka]] | Basket of conduct |

The edition of the [[Sixth Council]] adds three further works: the Nettippakaraṇa, the Peṭakopadesa and the [[Milindapañha]].

[^dha]: *Sumaṅgalavilāsinī*, the commentary to the Dīgha Nikāya, introduction (*nidānakathā*).
