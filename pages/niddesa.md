The **Niddesa** ({{lang|Pāli|Niddesa}}), "Exposition", is the eleventh book of the [[Khuddaka Nikāya]]. It is a commentary contained within the canon itself, explaining parts of the [[Sutta Nipāta]] word by word. Theravāda tradition ascribes it to the Venerable [[Sāriputta]].

It has two parts:

- **The Great Exposition** ({{term|Mahāniddesa}}), on the Chapter of Octads ({{term|Aṭṭhakavagga}}).
- **The Lesser Exposition** ({{term|Cūḷaniddesa}}), on the Way to the Beyond ({{term|Pārāyanavagga}}) and the Rhinoceros Sutta.
