**Citta** ({{lang|Pāli|Citta}}) is the first month of the Theravāda lunar calendar in the traditional Pāli listing, falling in about March to April in the Gregorian calendar. Like every month of the calendar, it takes its name from the constellation ({{term|nakkhatta}}) near which the moon is full in that month. It is also known by the name **Rammaka**, "the delightful month".[^abh]

## In the calendar

Citta opens the traditional Pāli list of the twelve months and belongs to the hot season ({{term|gimhāna}}).[^abh] In Myanmar its equivalent, Tagu, is the first month of the year, and the traditional New Year falls in it. It has 29 days, follows [[Phagguṇa]] and is followed by [[Vesākha]]. Like every month, it contains four uposatha observance days: the eighth day of the waxing moon, the full moon, the eighth day of the waning moon and the new moon.
