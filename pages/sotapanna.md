A **sotāpanna** ({{lang|Pāli|sotāpanna}}) is a stream-enterer: one who has entered the stream ({{term|sota}}) of the [[Noble Eightfold Path]] that flows inevitably towards [[Nibbāna]]. Stream-entry is the first of the four noble stages ([[Ariya-puggala]]).[^sn5511]

## Fetters abandoned

The stream-enterer has abandoned three fetters ([[Saṃyojana]]): personality view, doubt, and clinging to rules and observances. A stream-enterer is no longer subject to rebirth in the states of loss and will attain final Nibbāna within at most seven more lives.[^sn5511]

## The four factors

The four factors of the stream-enterer are unwavering confidence in the Buddha, the Dhamma and the Saṅgha, and the virtue dear to the noble ones, unbroken and leading to concentration.[^sn5511] The four factors leading to stream-entry are association with good people, hearing the true Dhamma, wise attention, and practice in accordance with the Dhamma.

## The vision of the Dhamma

The attainment of stream-entry is described as the arising of the dust-free, stainless vision of the Dhamma: "Whatever is subject to arising is subject to cessation." This was the vision that arose in the Venerable [[Aññā Koṇḍañña]] while the [[Dhammacakkappavattana Sutta]] was being spoken.[^sn5611]

## Incapable of six wrongs

The [[Ratana Sutta]] says that the stream-enterer is incapable of the six grave wrongs: matricide, patricide, killing an arahant, wounding a Buddha, causing a schism in the Saṅgha, and taking another teacher. The stream-enterer is also incapable of concealing a wrong done.

[^sn5511]: Saṃyutta Nikāya 55, *Sotāpatti Saṃyutta*.
[^sn5611]: Saṃyutta Nikāya 56.11, *Dhammacakkappavattana Sutta*.
