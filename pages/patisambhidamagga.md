The **Paṭisambhidāmagga** ({{lang|Pāli|Paṭisambhidāmagga}}), "the Path of Discrimination", is the twelfth book of the [[Khuddaka Nikāya]]. Theravāda tradition ascribes it to the Venerable [[Sāriputta]]. It consists of treatises ({{term|kathā}}) analysing topics of the teaching in a systematic way close to that of the Abhidhamma.

Its first treatise, the Treatise on Knowledge ({{term|Ñāṇakathā}}), lists and explains seventy-three kinds of knowledge, beginning with the knowledge that comes from learning. Other treatises deal with views, mindfulness of breathing, the faculties, liberation, the four truths and loving-kindness.

The [[Visuddhimagga]] draws on the Paṭisambhidāmagga throughout, especially for its account of insight, and quotes it often.
