The **Majjhima Nikāya** ({{lang|Pāli|Majjhima Nikāya}}), "the Middle-length Discourses", is the second of the five collections of the [[Sutta Piṭaka]]. It contains 152 discourses of middle length.[^dha] According to the commentaries it was entrusted after the [[First Council]] to the pupils of the Venerable [[Sāriputta]].[^dha]

## Divisions

| Division | Suttas |
|---|---|
| {{term|Mūlapaṇṇāsa}}, the Root Fifty | MN 1–50 |
| {{term|Majjhimapaṇṇāsa}}, the Middle Fifty | MN 51–100 |
| {{term|Uparipaṇṇāsa}}, the Final Fifty | MN 101–152 |

Each fifty is divided into five chapters ({{term|vagga}}) of about ten suttas.

## Notable suttas

The collection includes the [[Mūlapariyāya Sutta]], [[Sabbāsava Sutta]], [[Satipaṭṭhāna Sutta]], [[Alagaddūpama Sutta]], [[Ariyapariyesana Sutta]], [[Aṅgulimāla Sutta]], [[Ānāpānassati Sutta]] and [[Saccavibhaṅga Sutta]], among many others.

[^dha]: *Sumaṅgalavilāsinī*, the commentary to the Dīgha Nikāya, introduction (*nidānakathā*).
