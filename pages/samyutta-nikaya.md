The **Saṃyutta Nikāya** ({{lang|Pāli|Saṃyutta Nikāya}}), "the Connected Discourses", is the third of the five collections of the [[Sutta Piṭaka]]. Its discourses are grouped by subject into fifty-six "connected" groups ({{term|saṃyutta}}), which are arranged in five major divisions ({{term|vagga}}). The commentaries count 7,762 discourses in it.[^dha] Many of the most important teachings of the Blessed One are found here, including the first two discourses, the [[Dhammacakkappavattana Sutta]] and the [[Anattalakkhaṇa Sutta]].

## The five divisions

| Division | Pāli | Groups | Content |
|---|---|---|---|
| The Book with Verses | {{term|Sagāthāvagga}} | 11 (SN 1–11) | Discourses containing verses, many spoken to devas, Māra, brahmins and kings |
| The Book of Causation | {{term|Nidānavagga}} | 10 (SN 12–21) | Dependent origination, the elements, the round of rebirth, the Venerable Mahākassapa, gains and honour, and similes |
| The Book of the Aggregates | {{term|Khandhavagga}} | 13 (SN 22–34) | The five aggregates and related topics |
| The Book of the Six Sense Bases | {{term|Saḷāyatanavagga}} | 10 (SN 35–44) | The six sense bases, feeling, and Nibbāna |
| The Great Book | {{term|Mahāvagga}} | 12 (SN 45–56) | The factors of the path to awakening and the Four Noble Truths |

## Important groups

Among the fifty-six groups are:

| No. | Group | Subject |
|---|---|---|
| SN 12 | {{term|Nidāna Saṃyutta}} | Dependent origination |
| SN 15 | {{term|Anamatagga Saṃyutta}} | The round of rebirth without discoverable beginning |
| SN 22 | {{term|Khandha Saṃyutta}} | The five aggregates, including the Anattalakkhaṇa Sutta (SN 22.59) |
| SN 35 | {{term|Saḷāyatana Saṃyutta}} | The six internal and external sense bases |
| SN 36 | {{term|Vedanā Saṃyutta}} | Feeling |
| SN 38 | {{term|Jambukhādaka Saṃyutta}} | Questions of the wanderer Jambukhādaka to the Venerable Sāriputta, including "What is Nibbāna?" |
| SN 43 | {{term|Asaṅkhata Saṃyutta}} | The unconditioned and its thirty-three names |
| SN 45 | {{term|Magga Saṃyutta}} | The [[Noble Eightfold Path]] |
| SN 46 | {{term|Bojjhaṅga Saṃyutta}} | The seven factors of awakening |
| SN 47 | {{term|Satipaṭṭhāna Saṃyutta}} | The four foundations of mindfulness |
| SN 56 | {{term|Sacca Saṃyutta}} | The [[Four Noble Truths]], including the Dhammacakkappavattana Sutta (SN 56.11) |

## Transmission

At the First Council the Venerable Ānanda recited the discourses. According to the commentaries, the Saṃyutta Nikāya was then entrusted to the pupils of the Venerable Mahākassapa to preserve by recitation.[^dha] Its commentary, the *Sāratthappakāsinī*, was compiled in Pāli by the Venerable Buddhaghosa.
