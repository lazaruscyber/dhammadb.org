The **Abhidhamma Piṭaka** ({{lang|Pāli|Abhidhamma Piṭaka}}), "the Basket of Higher Teaching", is the third division of the [[Tipiṭaka]]. Where the suttas teach in conventional language suited to particular listeners, the Abhidhamma analyses experience in terms of ultimate realities ({{term|paramattha}}): [[Citta|consciousness]], [[Cetasika|mental factors]], [[Rūpa|matter]] and [[Nibbāna]].

## Origin

According to the commentaries, the Blessed One taught the Abhidhamma in the Tāvatiṃsa heaven during the seventh rains residence after the awakening, for the benefit of the deva who had been the Bodhisatta's mother. Each day the Blessed One came down to Lake Anotatta and gave the Venerable [[Sāriputta]] a summary of what had been taught; Sāriputta then taught it to five hundred pupils.[^as]

|---|---|

| [[Vibhaṅga]] | Analysis of eighteen topics |

| [[Puggalapaññatti]] | Designation of individuals |
| [[Kathāvatthu]] | Points of controversy |
| [[Yamaka]] | The book of pairs |
| [[Paṭṭhāna]] | Conditional relations |

## Study

In Myanmar the Abhidhamma is studied widely by bhikkhus and lay people alike, usually beginning with the manual [[Abhidhammattha Saṅgaha]].

[^as]: *Atthasālinī*, the commentary to the Dhammasaṅgaṇī, introduction.
