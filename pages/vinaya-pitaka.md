The **Vinaya Piṭaka** ({{lang|Pāli|Vinaya Piṭaka}}), "the Basket of Discipline", is the first of the three divisions of the [[Tipiṭaka]]. It contains the training rules laid down by the Blessed One for bhikkhus and nuns, the stories of how each rule came to be laid down, and the procedures by which the [[Saṅgha]] conducts its affairs.

The Vinaya is called the life of the dispensation: the commentaries say that as long as the Vinaya endures, the dispensation endures.[^sp]

## Divisions

| Division | Contents |
|---|---|
| [[Suttavibhaṅga]] | Analysis of the rules of the [[Pātimokkha]] for bhikkhus and for nuns |
| [[Khandhaka]] | The Mahāvagga and Cullavagga: procedures for ordination, the [[Uposatha]], the [[Vassa|rains residence]], robes, medicine and other matters |
| [[Parivāra]] | A summary and catechism of the whole Vinaya, arranged for study |

In the edition of the [[Sixth Council]] it is printed in five books: Pārājika, Pācittiya, Mahāvagga, Cullavagga and Parivāra.

## Origin

The training rules were not laid down all at once. The Blessed One laid them down one by one as occasions arose, each in response to a particular case of misconduct, for the well-being of the Saṅgha and the long endurance of the true Dhamma.[^vin] At the [[First Council]] the Venerable Mahākassapa questioned the Venerable [[Upāli]], foremost in the Vinaya, on each rule, and the Vinaya was recited by the assembly.[^cv11]
