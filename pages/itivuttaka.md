The **Itivuttaka** ({{lang|Pāli|Itivuttaka}}), "Thus It Was Said", is the fourth book of the [[Khuddaka Nikāya]]. It contains 112 short discourses, each combining prose and verse, arranged in four books from ones to fours. Each begins with the phrase "This was said by the Blessed One, said by the Arahant, so I heard."

According to the commentary, the discourses were heard by the laywoman Khujjuttarā, a servant of Queen Sāmāvatī of Kosambī, who went to hear the Blessed One each day and repeated the teachings to the queen and her attendants.[^ita] The Blessed One declared Khujjuttarā foremost among the laywomen who were widely learned.[^an1]

The Itivuttaka includes the discourse on the two elements of [[Nibbāna]] (Iti 44).

[^ita]: *Paramatthadīpanī*, the commentary to the Itivuttaka.
[^an1]: Aṅguttara Nikāya 1, *Etadaggavagga* (the foremost disciples).
