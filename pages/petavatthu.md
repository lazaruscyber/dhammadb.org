The **Petavatthu** ({{lang|Pāli|Petavatthu}}), "Stories of the Departed", is the seventh book of the [[Khuddaka Nikāya]]. Its verse stories tell of beings reborn as departed ones ({{term|peta}}), who suffer hunger, thirst and other torments because of miserliness, deceit or other misdeeds in their previous lives.

A recurring theme is the dedication of merit: when relatives give gifts to the Saṅgha and dedicate the merit to a departed being, the being rejoices and is relieved. The book includes the Tirokuṭṭa Sutta, which is also found in the [[Khuddakapāṭha]] and is recited at Theravāda ceremonies for the departed.
