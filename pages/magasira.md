**Māgasira** ({{lang|Pāli|Māgasira}}) is the ninth month of the Theravāda lunar calendar in the traditional Pāli listing, falling in about November to December in the Gregorian calendar. Like every month of the calendar, it takes its name from the constellation ({{term|nakkhatta}}) near which the moon is full in that month.

## In the calendar

Māgasira is the first month of the cold season ({{term|hemanta}}), which begins after the end of the rains. It has 29 days, follows [[Kattikā]] and is followed by [[Phussa]]. Like every month, it contains four uposatha observance days: the eighth day of the waxing moon, the full moon, the eighth day of the waning moon and the new moon.

## Events

Sri Lankan tradition, following the chronicles, holds that the Therī Saṅghamittā, daughter of the emperor Asoka, arrived in the island on the full moon of this month, bringing a branch of the Bodhi tree under which the Blessed One attained awakening. The full moon is observed in Sri Lanka as Unduvap Poya.[^mhv19]
