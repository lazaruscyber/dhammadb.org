The **Papañcasūdanī** ({{lang|Pāli|Papañcasūdanī}}), "Destroyer of Proliferation", is the commentary to the [[Majjhima Nikāya]], compiled in Pāli by the Venerable [[Buddhaghosa]]. It explains the 152 discourses of the collection, giving the background of each, the meaning of its words and the purpose of its teaching. Its commentary on the [[Satipaṭṭhāna Sutta]] is one of the main traditional sources for the practice of mindfulness.
