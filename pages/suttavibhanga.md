The **Suttavibhaṅga** ({{lang|Pāli|Suttavibhaṅga}}) is the first part of the [[Vinaya Piṭaka]]. It takes each rule of the [[Pātimokkha]] in turn and explains it. It has two sections: the Great Analysis ({{term|Mahāvibhaṅga}}), on the rules for bhikkhus, and the Analysis for Nuns ({{term|Bhikkhunīvibhaṅga}}).[^vin]

## Method

For each rule the Suttavibhaṅga gives:[^vin]

1. **The origin story**, telling the incident that led the Blessed One to lay down the rule, and often later amendments.
2. **The rule** itself, as recited in the Pātimokkha.
3. **A word-by-word analysis** of the rule.
4. **The cases**, showing when the rule is broken, when it is not, and the exemptions.

## Arrangement

The rules are arranged in the same classes as the Pātimokkha, beginning with the four rules of defeat ([[Pārājika]]) and the thirteen rules requiring a meeting of the Saṅgha ([[Saṅghādisesa]]). In the edition of the [[Sixth Council]] the Suttavibhaṅga fills two volumes, called Pārājika and Pācittiya after their contents.

[^vin]: Vinaya Piṭaka, Suttavibhaṅga.
