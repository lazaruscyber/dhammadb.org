The **Parivāra** ({{lang|Pāli|Parivāra}}), "the Accessory", is the third and last part of the [[Vinaya Piṭaka]]. It is a manual for the study of the Vinaya, summarising and classifying the material of the [[Suttavibhaṅga]] and [[Khandhaka]] in the form of questions and answers, so that the rules can be learned, remembered and applied.

It records, for each rule, where it was laid down, concerning whom, and on what matter, and it groups offences and procedures in numerical lists. The Parivāra also contains the verse, often quoted in Theravāda teaching, that all formations are impermanent, that the conditioned are suffering and non-self, and that [[Nibbāna]] and concepts too are non-self.[^pv]
