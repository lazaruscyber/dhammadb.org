The **thirty-one planes of existence** ({{lang|Pāli|bhūmi}}) are the realms in which beings are reborn in [[Saṃsāra|saṃsāra]], according to their [[Kamma|kamma]]. The scheme is set out in the [[Abhidhammattha Saṅgaha]] on the basis of the suttas and the [[Vibhaṅga]].[^abhs]

## The planes

| Sphere | Plane | Number |
|---|---|---|
| Sense sphere, states of loss | Hell ({{term|niraya}}), animal realm, realm of ghosts ({{term|peta}}), realm of titans ({{term|asura}}) | 4 |
| Sense sphere, happy destinations | Human realm, and six heavens: Cātumahārājika, Tāvatiṃsa, Yāma, Tusita, Nimmānarati, Paranimmitavasavatti | 7 |
| Fine-material sphere | Sixteen Brahmā realms, including the five Pure Abodes | 16 |
| Immaterial sphere | Infinite space, infinite consciousness, nothingness, neither perception nor non-perception | 4 |

## How beings are reborn there

Rebirth in the four states of loss results from unwholesome kamma. Rebirth as a human or in the six heavens results from wholesome sense-sphere kamma such as [[Dāna|giving]] and [[Sīla|virtue]]. Rebirth in the fine-material realms results from attaining the [[Jhāna|jhānas]], and rebirth in the immaterial realms from the immaterial attainments. The five Pure Abodes ({{term|suddhāvāsa}}) are inhabited only by non-returners ([[Anāgāmī]]).[^abhs]

One fine-material plane, that of non-percipient beings ({{term|asaññasatta}}), has matter only and no mind.

## Impermanence of all planes

No plane is eternal. Even the life span of the highest plane, 84,000 great aeons, comes to an end. The Blessed One said that just as even a tiny amount of excrement smells bad, so even a moment of existence is not to be praised.[^an1] The only escape from all the planes is [[Nibbāna]].

See also [[Deva]], [[Brahmā]].

[^abhs]: Anuruddha, *Abhidhammattha Saṅgaha*. Chapter V.
[^an1]: Aṅguttara Nikāya 1, the Book of Ones, *Ekadhamma* section.
