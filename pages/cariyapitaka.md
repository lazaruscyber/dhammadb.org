The **Cariyāpiṭaka** ({{lang|Pāli|Cariyāpiṭaka}}), "Basket of Conduct", is the fifteenth book of the [[Khuddaka Nikāya]]. In thirty-five short verse stories, told in the first person, the Blessed One recalls past lives in which the [[Pāramī|perfections]] were fulfilled, including generosity, virtue, renunciation, resolution, truthfulness, loving-kindness and equanimity.

Many of its stories are also found in the [[Jātaka]], including the life of Vessantara as an example of the perfection of generosity.
