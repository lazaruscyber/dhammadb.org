Pages about the Theravāda lunar calendar, by which the uposatha days, the rains residence and the great festivals are reckoned.

The year has twelve lunar months of 29 or 30 days, each named after the constellation ({{term|nakkhatta}}) near which the moon is full. The months are grouped into three seasons of four months: the hot season ({{term|gimhāna}}), the rainy season ({{term|vassāna}}) and the cold season ({{term|hemanta}}). In leap years an extra month, the second [[Āsāḷha]], is added so that the calendar keeps pace with the seasons.
