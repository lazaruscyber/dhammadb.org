The **Vimānavatthu** ({{lang|Pāli|Vimānavatthu}}), "Stories of the Mansions", is the sixth book of the [[Khuddaka Nikāya]]. It consists of stories in verse in which a [[Deva|deva]], usually questioned by the Venerable [[Mahāmoggallāna]], describes the splendid celestial mansion ({{term|vimāna}}) in which they live, and the good deed done in their human life that brought them there.

The deeds are often small: a gift of food, a seat, a flower or a lamp, given with a confident heart. The book illustrates the teaching on [[Kamma|kamma]] and its results, and encourages generosity ([[Dāna]]) and virtue.
