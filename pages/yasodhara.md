**Yasodharā** ({{lang|Pāli|Yasodharā}}) was the wife of the Bodhisatta Siddhattha and the mother of [[Rāhula]]. In the suttas and commentaries she is usually called Rāhulamātā, "the mother of Rāhula". As the nun Bhaddakaccānā, she was declared foremost among nuns of great supernormal knowledge.[^an1]

## Past lives

The commentaries teach that she was the companion of the Bodhisatta through many lives. In the time of the Buddha Dīpaṅkara, as the maiden Sumittā, she gave lotus flowers to the hermit Sumedha to offer to the Buddha, and resolved to be the companion of Sumedha until the awakening.[^bv]

## The going forth of the Bodhisatta

On the night of the going forth, the Bodhisatta went to her room, saw her asleep with the newborn Rāhula, and left without waking her, resolving to return as a Buddha.[^jn]

## Devotion

When she heard the Bodhisatta wore yellow robes, she wore yellow; when she heard the Bodhisatta ate one meal a day, she did the same, and gave up garlands and high beds. When the Blessed One returned to [[Kapilavatthu]], she would not go out to greet the Blessed One, saying that if she had any virtue the Blessed One would come to her. The Blessed One came to her apartment with the two chief disciples, and she fell at the Blessed One's feet. The Blessed One then told King [[Suddhodana]] the Candakinnara Jātaka of her past devotion.[^jn]

## Going forth

She later went forth under [[Mahāpajāpatī Gotamī]] and attained arahantship.

[^bv]: *Buddhavaṃsa* commentary, *Madhuratthavilāsinī*.
