The **Therīgāthā** ({{lang|Pāli|Therīgāthā}}), "Verses of the Elder Nuns", is the ninth book of the [[Khuddaka Nikāya]]. It preserves the verses of about seventy elder nuns, in which they tell of their lives, their struggles and their liberation.

Among the nuns whose verses are preserved are [[Mahāpajāpatī Gotamī]], [[Khemā]], [[Uppalavaṇṇā]], [[Paṭācārā]] and [[Kisāgotamī]]. Their stories, told more fully in the commentary, show women from every walk of life attaining arahantship.
