The **Jātaka** ({{lang|Pāli|Jātaka}}), "Birth Stories", is the tenth book of the [[Khuddaka Nikāya]]. It tells of the past lives of the Bodhisatta, during which the [[Pāramī|perfections]] were fulfilled. In the canon only the verses are preserved; the prose stories that frame them are given in the commentary, the *Jātakaṭṭhakathā*.[^jn]

## Structure

The 547 stories are arranged in books ({{term|nipāta}}) according to the number of verses they contain. Each story in the commentary has a set form: a present story, telling the occasion on which the Blessed One related it; the past story itself; and a connection, identifying who in the present was who in the past.

The commentary is introduced by the *Nidānakathā*, a life of the Bodhisatta from the aspiration at the feet of the Buddha Dīpaṅkara to the founding of the Jetavana monastery.[^jn]

## The ten great birth stories

The last ten stories, the Great Book ({{term|Mahānipāta}}), are especially honoured, each illustrating one of the ten perfections. The last of all is the Vessantara Jātaka, the story of the Bodhisatta's perfection of generosity, which is widely recited and depicted in Theravāda countries.

[^jn]: Jātaka Commentary, *Nidānakathā*.
