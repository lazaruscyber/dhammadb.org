**Vassa** ({{lang|Pāli|vassa}}), "rain", is the annual three-month rains residence during which bhikkhus remain in one dwelling. It begins on the day after the full moon of [[Āsāḷha]] and ends on the full moon of [[Assayuja]], when the [[Pavāraṇā]] is held.[^vin]

## Origin

The Vinaya records that people criticised the bhikkhus for wandering during the rains, trampling young crops and harming small creatures, while even birds stay in their nests. The Blessed One then laid down the rule that bhikkhus must enter the rains residence.[^vin]

## Rules

A bhikkhu must determine to stay in one residence for the three months. He may leave for up to seven days on account of certain duties, such as visiting a sick bhikkhu or a supporter who has invited him. If dangers arise, such as wild animals, fire or the loss of supporters, he may leave without breaking the residence. There is also a later rains residence, beginning one month after the first.[^vin]

## Seniority

A bhikkhu's seniority is counted in rains ({{term|vassa}}) since his higher ordination. In Myanmar a bhikkhu is often asked "how many vassa?".

## Lay practice

For lay people, the rains is a time of increased practice. Many keep the eight precepts on every [[Uposatha]], attend more Dhamma talks and give offerings. Marriages and other festivities are traditionally postponed in Myanmar until after the rains. The rains end with the Pavāraṇā and are followed by the [[Kathina]] season.

[^vin]: Vinaya Piṭaka, Mahāvagga 3, *Vassūpanāyikakkhandhaka*.
