The **Udāna** ({{lang|Pāli|Udāna}}), "Inspired Utterances", is the third book of the [[Khuddaka Nikāya]]. It contains eighty short suttas in eight chapters. Each tells of an occasion on which the Blessed One, moved by deep feeling or understanding, spoke an inspired utterance, usually in verse.

## Contents

The first chapter opens with the Blessed One at the foot of the Bodhi tree after the awakening, reflecting on dependent origination. The eighth chapter contains the well-known passages on [[Nibbāna]], including the declaration: "There is, bhikkhus, an unborn, unbecome, unmade, unconditioned."[^ud83] The book also contains the simile of the blind men and the elephant (Ud 6.4) and the account of the Blessed One's last meal (Ud 8.5).

[^ud83]: Udāna 8.3.
