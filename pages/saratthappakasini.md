The **Sāratthappakāsinī** ({{lang|Pāli|Sāratthappakāsinī}}), "Revealer of the Essential Meaning", is the commentary to the [[Saṃyutta Nikāya]], compiled in Pāli by the Venerable [[Buddhaghosa]]. It explains the discourses of the five divisions of the collection, including the [[Dhammacakkappavattana Sutta]], and is the source for many details of the first discourse and of the lives of the disciples.
