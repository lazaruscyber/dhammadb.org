The **Ānāpānassati Sutta** ({{lang|Pāli|Ānāpānassati Sutta}}), "the Discourse on Mindfulness of Breathing", is the 118th discourse of the [[Majjhima Nikāya]].

## Occasion

At the end of the rains residence many senior bhikkhus were teaching newer bhikkhus. Pleased with their progress, the Blessed One extended the stay for another month, until the {{term|komudī}} full moon of [[Kattikā]]. On that full moon the Blessed One praised the assembly of bhikkhus and taught mindfulness of breathing.[^mn]

## The sixteen steps

The steps are given in four groups of four:

| Group | Steps |
|---|---|
| Body | Breathing long; breathing short; experiencing the whole body; calming bodily formation |
| Feeling | Experiencing rapture; experiencing happiness; experiencing mental formation; calming mental formation |
| Mind | Experiencing the mind; gladdening the mind; concentrating the mind; liberating the mind |
| Phenomena | Contemplating impermanence; fading away; cessation; relinquishment |

## Fulfilment

Mindfulness of breathing, developed and cultivated, fulfils the four foundations of mindfulness; these fulfil the seven factors of awakening; and these fulfil true knowledge and liberation.[^mn]
