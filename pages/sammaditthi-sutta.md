The **Sammādiṭṭhi Sutta** ({{lang|Pāli|Sammādiṭṭhi Sutta}}), "the Discourse on Right View", is the ninth discourse of the [[Majjhima Nikāya]], spoken by the Venerable [[Sāriputta]]. In answer to the question "In what way is one a person of right view, who has arrived at this true Dhamma?", Sāriputta gives sixteen ways of understanding.[^mn]

## The sixteen ways

In each way the noble disciple understands a subject, its origin, its cessation and the way to its cessation:

:::columns 2
1. The wholesome and its roots, the unwholesome and its roots
2. Nutriment
3. The Four Noble Truths
4. Ageing and death
5. Birth
6. Existence
7. Clinging
8. Craving
9. Feeling
10. Contact
11. The six sense bases
12. Mentality-materiality
13. Consciousness
14. Formations
15. Ignorance
16. The taints
:::

Items four to fifteen follow the links of [[Paṭicca-samuppāda|dependent origination]]. The commentary considers this sutta a summary of the whole teaching, and it is traditionally studied with great care.

[^mn]: Majjhima Nikāya 9, *Sammādiṭṭhi Sutta*.
