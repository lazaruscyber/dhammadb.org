The **Apadāna** ({{lang|Pāli|Apadāna}}), "Stories of Past Deeds", is the thirteenth book of the [[Khuddaka Nikāya]]. In its verse accounts, arahant bhikkhus and nuns tell of the meritorious deeds they performed in past lives, often in the presence of an earlier Buddha, and of the results of those deeds that led them in the end to meet the Blessed One and attain liberation.

It has four sections: the Buddha-apadāna, the Paccekabuddha-apadāna, the Thera-apadāna (elder bhikkhus) and the Therī-apadāna (elder nuns). It is an important source for the lives of the disciples, alongside the [[Theragāthā]], [[Therīgāthā]] and their commentaries.
