The **Vibhaṅga** ({{lang|Pāli|Vibhaṅga}}), "the Book of Analysis", is the second book of the [[Abhidhamma Piṭaka]]. It contains eighteen chapters, each analysing a topic of the teaching. Most chapters treat their topic in three ways: by the method of the suttas ({{term|suttanta-bhājaniya}}), by the method of the Abhidhamma ({{term|abhidhamma-bhājaniya}}), and by questions and answers ({{term|pañhāpucchaka}}).[^vbh]

## The eighteen chapters

:::columns 3
1. Aggregates ([[Khandha]])
2. Bases ([[Āyatana]])
3. Elements ([[Dhātu]])
4. Truths ([[Four Noble Truths|sacca]])
5. Faculties ([[Indriya]])
6. Dependent origination ([[Paṭicca-samuppāda]])
7. Foundations of mindfulness ([[Satipaṭṭhāna]])
8. Right efforts ([[Sammappadhāna]])
9. Bases of power ([[Iddhipāda]])
10. Factors of awakening ([[Bojjhaṅga]])
11. The path ([[Noble Eightfold Path|magga]])
12. Absorptions ([[Jhāna]])
13. Illimitables ({{term|appamaññā}})
14. Training rules
15. Analytical knowledges
16. Knowledge
17. Minor matters
18. Heart of the Dhamma
:::

Its commentary is the *Sammohavinodanī*, "Dispeller of Delusion".
