The **Khandhaka** ({{lang|Pāli|Khandhaka}}) is the second part of the [[Vinaya Piṭaka]]. It is made up of two books, the **Mahāvagga** and the **Cullavagga**, which together contain twenty-two chapters ({{term|khandhaka}}) on the procedures and customs of the monastic community.

## Mahāvagga

The Mahāvagga opens with a narrative of the events after the awakening: the weeks at the Bodhi tree, the decision to teach, the [[Dhammacakkappavattana Sutta|first discourse]], the [[Anattalakkhaṇa Sutta|second discourse]], and the growth of the Saṅgha. Its chapters then cover ordination ([[Upasampadā]]), the [[Uposatha]], the [[Vassa|rains residence]], the [[Pavāraṇā]], footwear, medicines, the [[Kathina]], robes, and the formal acts of the Saṅgha.[^mv]

## Cullavagga

The Cullavagga deals with disciplinary acts, probation, the settling of disputes, lodgings, schism, duties of bhikkhus to one another, and the ordination of nuns. Its last two chapters give the accounts of the [[First Council]] at Rājagaha and the [[Second Council]] at Vesālī.[^cv]
