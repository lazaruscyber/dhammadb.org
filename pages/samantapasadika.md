The **Samantapāsādikā** ({{lang|Pāli|Samantapāsādikā}}), "the All-Pleasing", is the commentary to the [[Vinaya Piṭaka]], compiled in Pāli by the Venerable [[Buddhaghosa]] from the ancient commentaries preserved in Sri Lanka.

It explains each training rule and procedure of the Vinaya and settles many questions of practice. Its long introduction ({{term|Bāhiranidāna}}) gives the history of the [[First Council|First]], [[Second Council|Second]] and [[Third Council|Third]] Councils, the missions sent out after the Third Council, and the establishment of the teaching in Sri Lanka by the Venerable [[Mahinda]]. The Vinaya-study of Theravāda bhikkhus relies on it closely.
