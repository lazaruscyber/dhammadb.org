The **Third Council** ({{lang|Pāli|Tatiya Saṅgāyana}}) was held at the Asokārāma in Pāṭaliputta under the patronage of the emperor [[Asoka]], presided over by the Venerable Moggaliputta Tissa.[^sp]

## Cause

Because of the emperor's generosity, many followers of other doctrines put on robes to share in the gain, while holding their own views. The true bhikkhus would not hold the [[Uposatha]] with them, and for seven years no Uposatha was held at the Asokārāma. A minister sent by Asoka to restore it beheaded several bhikkhus who refused. Distressed, the emperor asked Moggaliputta Tissa, who came from his retreat on the Ahogaṅgā mountain.[^sp]

## The purification

Asoka had the bhikkhus questioned one by one in the elder's presence: "What did the Fully Enlightened One teach?" Those who gave the views of other schools were disrobed. Those who answered that the Blessed One was a {{term|vibhajjavādī}}, "one who teaches by analysis", were recognised as true bhikkhus. The Saṅgha was then purified and the Uposatha held.[^sp]

## The recitation and the Kathāvatthu

A thousand arahants recited the Dhamma and Vinaya over nine months. Moggaliputta Tissa composed the [[Kathāvatthu]], refuting wrong views, on the basis of the outline given by the Blessed One.[^sp]

## The missions

At the end of the Council, missions were sent to nine regions:

| Elder | Region |
|---|---|
| Majjhantika | Kasmīra and Gandhāra |
| Mahādeva | Mahisamaṇḍala |
| Rakkhita | Vanavāsī |
| Yonaka Dhammarakkhita | Aparantaka |
| Mahādhammarakkhita | Mahāraṭṭha |
| Mahārakkhita | The Yona country |
| Majjhima | The Himalaya region |
| Soṇa and Uttara | Suvaṇṇabhūmi |
| [[Mahinda]] | Laṅkā (Sri Lanka) |

[^sp]: *Samantapāsādikā*, introduction; *Mahāvaṃsa*. Chapters V and XII.
