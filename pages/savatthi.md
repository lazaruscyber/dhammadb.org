**Sāvatthī** ({{lang|Pāli|Sāvatthī}}) was the capital of the kingdom of Kosala, ruled in the Blessed One's time by King Pasenadi. More suttas were taught at Sāvatthī than anywhere else.[^dhpa]

## The rains residences

According to the commentaries, the Blessed One spent twenty-five rains residences at Sāvatthī: nineteen at the [[Jetavana]], donated by [[Anāthapiṇḍika]], and six at the Eastern Park, donated by [[Visākhā]].[^dhpa]

## King Pasenadi

King Pasenadi became a lay follower early in the Blessed One's teaching. The Kosala Saṃyutta of the [[Saṃyutta Nikāya]] records many conversations between the king and the Blessed One. In the Dhammacetiya Sutta, the king explains his reverence for the Blessed One, comparing the harmony of the bhikkhus to the quarrels of kings, brahmins and householders.[^mn89]

## The twin miracle

The commentaries relate that at Sāvatthī, at the foot of a mango tree by the city gate, the Blessed One performed the twin miracle ({{term|yamakapāṭihāriya}}), causing fire and water to stream from the body at once, to humble rival teachers. The Blessed One then went to the Tāvatiṃsa heaven to teach the Abhidhamma to the Bodhisatta's mother.[^dhpa]

## Discourses

Among the many suttas taught at Sāvatthī are the [[Sabbāsava Sutta]], the [[Alagaddūpama Sutta]], the [[Maṅgala Sutta]], the [[Aggañña Sutta]] and the [[Ānāpānassati Sutta]].

[^dhpa]: *Dhammapadaṭṭhakathā*, the commentary to the Dhammapada. On verse 181.
[^mn89]: Majjhima Nikāya 89, *Dhammacetiya Sutta*.
