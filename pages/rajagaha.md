**Rājagaha** ({{lang|Pāli|Rājagaha}}), "the king's house", was the capital of the kingdom of Magadha. It was ringed by five hills, among them the Vulture Peak ({{term|Gijjhakūṭa}}), a favourite dwelling of the Blessed One.[^vin]

## The Bodhisatta and Bimbisāra

The Bodhisatta passed through Rājagaha soon after going forth, and King [[Bimbisāra]] asked the Bodhisatta to return after awakening. The Blessed One did so, with a thousand bhikkhus, and the king gave the Bamboo Grove ([[Veḷuvana]]) as the first monastery.[^vin]

## The First Council

Three months after the parinibbāna, the [[First Council]] of 500 arahants was held at the Sattapaṇṇi cave on the slope of the Vebhāra hill, under the patronage of King Ajātasattu.[^vinc]

## The Vulture Peak

The commentaries explain the name either by the shape of the peak, like a vulture, or by the vultures that lived on it. Many suttas were taught there, including the [[Āṭānāṭiya Sutta]].
