The **Dhammasaṅgaṇī** ({{lang|Pāli|Dhammasaṅgaṇī}}), "Enumeration of Phenomena", is the first book of the [[Abhidhamma Piṭaka]]. It opens with the matrix ({{term|mātikā}}) of the whole Abhidhamma: a list of 22 triads ({{term|tika}}) and 100 dyads ({{term|duka}}) by which all phenomena can be classified. The first triad is wholesome ({{term|kusala}}), unwholesome ({{term|akusala}}) and indeterminate ({{term|abyākata}}).[^dhs]
