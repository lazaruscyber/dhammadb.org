The **Mahāvaṃsa** ({{lang|Pāli|Mahāvaṃsa}}), "the Great Chronicle", is a chronicle in Pāli verse of the history of Sri Lanka and of the Theravāda teaching there. It was composed by the Venerable Mahānāma of the Mahāvihāra, and was later continued by other authors in the work known as the *Cūḷavaṃsa*.

## Contents

The chronicle opens with the three visits of the Blessed One to Sri Lanka, and then records:

- the [[First Council|First]], [[Second Council|Second]] and [[Third Council|Third]] Councils;
- the reign of the emperor [[Asoka]];
- the coming of the Arahant [[Mahinda]] in the month of [[Jeṭṭha]] and the conversion of King Devānampiya Tissa;
- the arrival of the Therī [[Saṅghamittā]] with a branch of the Bodhi tree;
- the founding of the Mahāvihāra and the great shrines of [[Anurādhapura]];
- the reign of King Duṭṭhagāmaṇī;
- the writing down of the [[Tipiṭaka]] at the [[Fourth Council|Āluvihāra]].
