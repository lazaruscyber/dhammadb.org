The **Visuddhimagga** ({{lang|Pāli|Visuddhimagga}}), "the Path of Purification", is the great systematic manual of the Theravāda tradition. It was composed in Pāli by the Venerable [[Buddhaghosa]] at the Mahāvihāra in [[Anurādhapura]], Sri Lanka, and it presents the whole path to [[Nibbāna]] according to the teaching of the Mahāvihāra elders.[^mhv]

## Origin

According to the chronicle, when Buddhaghosa asked the Saṅgha of the Mahāvihāra for access to the commentaries, the elders gave him two verses to test his ability. In answer he composed the Visuddhimagga as a commentary on them. The elders accepted it as a summary of the whole teaching and gave him the commentaries to translate into Pāli.[^mhv]

## Framework

The whole work is an exposition of a single verse spoken by the Blessed One:

:::quote Saṃyutta Nikāya 1.23
*Sīle patiṭṭhāya naro sapañño, cittaṃ paññañca bhāvayaṃ;*
*ātāpī nipako bhikkhu, so imaṃ vijaṭaye jaṭaṃ.*

A wise person, established in virtue, developing the mind and wisdom, a bhikkhu ardent and discreet: such a one can untangle this tangle.
:::

## Parts

| Part | Chapters | Subject |
|---|---|---|
| [[Sīla|Virtue]] | I–II | Virtue and the ascetic practices ([[Dhutaṅga]]) |
| [[Samādhi|Concentration]] | III–XIII | The forty [[Kammaṭṭhāna|meditation subjects]], [[Jhāna|absorption]] and the supernormal powers |
| [[Paññā|Wisdom]] | XIV–XXIII | The soil of wisdom ([[Khandha|aggregates]], bases, elements, faculties, truths, [[Paṭicca-samuppāda|dependent origination]]), the [[Satta visuddhi|seven purifications]] and the benefits of wisdom |

## Authority

The Visuddhimagga is regarded in the Theravāda tradition as the authoritative exposition of the commentarial understanding of the path, and the commentaries frequently refer the reader to it.
