The **Pātimokkha** ({{lang|Pāli|Pātimokkha}}) is the code of training rules for fully ordained bhikkhus and nuns. It is recited by the [[Saṅgha]] on the [[Uposatha]] days of the new and full moon, after each bhikkhu has confessed any offences, so that the community remains pure.[^mv2] In the Theravāda tradition it has 227 rules for bhikkhus and 311 for nuns.

## The rules for bhikkhus

| Class | Pāli | Number | Consequence |
|---|---|---|---|
| Defeat | [[Pārājika]] | 4 | Loss of the status of bhikkhu for life |
| Meeting of the Saṅgha | [[Saṅghādisesa]] | 13 | Probation and rehabilitation by the Saṅgha |
| Undetermined | {{term|aniyata}} | 2 | Judged according to the facts |
| Forfeiture with expiation | {{term|nissaggiya pācittiya}} | 30 | The item is forfeited, then confession |
| Expiation | {{term|pācittiya}} | 92 | Confession |
| Acknowledgement | {{term|pāṭidesanīya}} | 4 | Acknowledgement |
| Training | {{term|sekhiya}} | 75 | Rules of etiquette and deportment |
| Settling disputes | {{term|adhikaraṇa-samatha}} | 7 | Procedures for settling legal issues |

## The two Pātimokkhas

The commentaries distinguish the **exhortation Pātimokkha** ({{term|ovāda-pātimokkha}}), the verses summing up the teaching of all Buddhas, from the **command Pātimokkha** ({{term|āṇā-pātimokkha}}), the code of rules. The exhortation includes the well-known verse: "Not doing any evil, undertaking the good, purifying one's mind: this is the teaching of the Buddhas."[^dhp]
