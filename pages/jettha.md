**Jeṭṭha** ({{lang|Pāli|Jeṭṭha}}) is the third month of the Theravāda lunar calendar in the traditional Pāli listing, falling in about May to June in the Gregorian calendar. Like every month of the calendar, it takes its name from the constellation ({{term|nakkhatta}}) near which the moon is full in that month.

## In the calendar

Jeṭṭha has 29 (30 in some leap years) days and belongs to the hot season ({{term|gimhāna}}), one of the three seasons of four months each into which the year is divided. It follows [[Vesākha]] and is followed by [[Āsāḷha]]. Like every month, it contains four uposatha observance days: the eighth day of the waxing moon, the full moon, the eighth day of the waning moon and the new moon.

## Events

According to the *Mahāvaṃsa*, the Arahant Mahinda, son of the emperor Asoka, arrived in Sri Lanka with his companions on the uposatha day of the month of Jeṭṭha, alighting on the Missaka mountain, the present Mihintale.[^mhv13] The full moon of Jeṭṭha is observed in Sri Lanka as Poson Poya, the day on which the Dhamma was established in the island.
