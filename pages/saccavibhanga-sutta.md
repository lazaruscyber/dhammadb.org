The **Saccavibhaṅga Sutta** ({{lang|Pāli|Saccavibhaṅga Sutta}}), "the Analysis of the Truths", is the 141st discourse of the [[Majjhima Nikāya]].

At Isipatana, where the Wheel of the Dhamma was first set in motion, the Blessed One recalled that teaching and recommended the Venerables [[Sāriputta]] and [[Mahāmoggallāna]] to the bhikkhus: Sāriputta is like a mother who gives birth, Moggallāna like a nurse who rears what is born. Sāriputta trains others to the fruit of stream-entry, Moggallāna to the supreme goal.[^mn]

After the Blessed One withdrew, Sāriputta explained the [[Four Noble Truths]] in detail, defining each term: birth, ageing, death, sorrow, lamentation, pain, grief, despair and the five aggregates subject to clinging; craving in its three kinds; the cessation of craving; and each factor of the [[Noble Eightfold Path]].[^mn]
