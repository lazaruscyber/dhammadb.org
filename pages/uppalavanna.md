The nun **Uppalavaṇṇā** ({{lang|Pāli|Uppalavaṇṇā}}), "having the colour of a blue lotus", was the second chief female disciple, foremost among the nuns in supernormal power.[^an1]

## Going forth

According to the commentary she was the daughter of a treasurer of [[Sāvatthī]], so beautiful that kings and merchants from all over India sought her hand. Her father, not wishing to offend any of them, asked whether she would go forth, and she agreed gladly. Soon after ordination, while gazing at the flame of a lamp as a fire [[Kasiṇa|kasiṇa]], she attained jhāna and then arahantship with the supernormal powers.[^tha]

## Facing Māra

The [[Therīgāthā]] and the Bhikkhunī Saṃyutta preserve her reply when Māra tried to frighten her as she stood alone at the foot of a sāla tree: "Though a hundred thousand rogues like you might come here, I stir not a hair, I feel no terror. Even alone, Māra, I do not fear you."[^sn15]

## The rule on seclusion

The Vinaya and commentary relate that while she was living alone in the Andhavana forest, she was assaulted. Following this, the Blessed One laid down that nuns should not dwell in the forest, and King Pasenadi had dwellings built for nuns within the city.[^dhpa]
