The **Mahāpadāna Sutta** ({{lang|Pāli|Mahāpadāna Sutta}}), "the Great Discourse on the Lineage", is the fourteenth discourse of the [[Dīgha Nikāya]]. Hearing the bhikkhus discussing past lives, the Blessed One told them about the seven Buddhas who had appeared in the last ninety-one aeons.[^dn14]

## The seven Buddhas

:::columns 4
1. Vipassī
2. Sikhī
3. Vessabhū
4. Kakusandha
5. Koṇāgamana
6. Kassapa
7. Gotama
:::

For each Buddha the sutta gives the aeon, the caste, the clan, the span of life, the tree of awakening, the chief disciples, the assemblies of disciples, the attendant, and the parents and city.

## The life of Vipassī

The sutta then tells at length the life of the Buddha Vipassī, showing the pattern followed by all Buddhas: descent into the mother's womb, birth, the thirty-two marks, the four sights of an old man, a sick man, a corpse and a recluse, the going forth, the awakening through insight into dependent origination, the hesitation to teach, the request of the Great Brahmā, and the first teaching. The sutta closes with the verses of the Ovāda Pātimokkha as recited by Vipassī Buddha.[^dn14]

[^dn14]: Dīgha Nikāya 14, *Mahāpadāna Sutta*.
