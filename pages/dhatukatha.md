The **Dhātukathā** ({{lang|Pāli|Dhātukathā}}), "Discourse on Elements", is the third book of the [[Abhidhamma Piṭaka]]. It examines each phenomenon of the matrix of the [[Dhammasaṅgaṇī]] in relation to the five aggregates ([[Khandha]]), the twelve bases ([[Āyatana]]) and the eighteen elements ([[Dhātu]]).[^dhk]

For each phenomenon it asks whether it is included ({{term|saṅgaha}}) or not included in each aggregate, base and element, and whether it is associated ({{term|sampayutta}}) or dissociated ({{term|vippayutta}}) with them. Its fourteen chapters work through these questions systematically, training the student to see each phenomenon precisely in its place.

[^dhk]: *Dhātukathā*.
