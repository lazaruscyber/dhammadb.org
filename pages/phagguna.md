**Phagguṇa** ({{lang|Pāli|Phagguṇa}}) is the twelfth month of the Theravāda lunar calendar in the traditional Pāli listing, falling in about February to March in the Gregorian calendar. Like every month of the calendar, it takes its name from the constellation ({{term|nakkhatta}}) near which the moon is full in that month.

## In the calendar

Phagguṇa has 30 days and belongs to the cold season ({{term|hemanta}}), one of the three seasons of four months each into which the year is divided. It follows [[Māgha]] and is followed by [[Citta]]. Like every month, it contains four uposatha observance days: the eighth day of the waxing moon, the full moon, the eighth day of the waning moon and the new moon.

## Events

According to the commentaries, King Suddhodana sent the minister Kāḷudāyī, a childhood companion born on the same day as the Bodhisatta, to invite the Blessed One to Kapilavatthu. Kāḷudāyī went forth, attained arahantship, and on the full-moon day of Phagguṇa, when the cold season had ended and spring had come, praised the beauty of the road and invited the Blessed One to make the journey. The Blessed One then set out for Kapilavatthu.[^jn][^mbv]
