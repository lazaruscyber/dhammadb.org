**Upasampadā** ({{lang|Pāli|upasampadā}}) is the higher ordination, by which a candidate becomes a fully ordained [[Bhikkhu|bhikkhu]].[^vin]

## Three historical forms

| Form | Description |
|---|---|
| "Come, bhikkhu" ({{term|ehibhikkhu-upasampadā}}) | Given by the Blessed One alone, with the words "Come, bhikkhu, the Dhamma is well proclaimed; live the holy life for the complete ending of suffering" |
| Going for the three refuges | Allowed for a time by the bhikkhus |
| Formal act with motion and three proclamations ({{term|ñatticatuttha-kamma}}) | Laid down by the Blessed One at [[Rājagaha]], and used ever since |

## The procedure

The ordination is held within a properly established [[Sīmā|boundary]]. In the middle country a quorum of at least ten bhikkhus is required, and in border regions five. The candidate must have a preceptor ({{term|upajjhāya}}), a bowl and robes, and is asked about the impediments: whether he is free of certain diseases, is a human being, a man, a free man, free of debt, not in the king's service, has his parents' permission, is at least twenty, and has bowl and robes.[^vin]

After the motion and three proclamations, if no bhikkhu objects, the candidate is ordained. The time is noted for seniority, and he is told the four supports (almsfood, rag robes, the foot of a tree and fermented urine as medicine) and the four things never to be done (sexual intercourse, theft, killing a human being, and falsely claiming superhuman states).[^vin]

[^vin]: Vinaya Piṭaka, Mahāvagga 1.
