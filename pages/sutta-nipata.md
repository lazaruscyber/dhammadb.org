The **Sutta Nipāta** ({{lang|Pāli|Sutta Nipāta}}), "the Group of Discourses", is the fifth book of the [[Khuddaka Nikāya]]. It is a collection of discourses, mostly in verse, arranged in five chapters.

## Chapters

| Chapter | Pāli | Notable suttas |
|---|---|---|
| The Snake | {{term|Uragavagga}} | The Khaggavisāṇa (Rhinoceros) Sutta, the Kasibhāradvāja Sutta, the [[Karaṇīyametta Sutta]] |
| The Minor Chapter | {{term|Cūḷavagga}} | The [[Ratana Sutta]], the [[Maṅgala Sutta]] |
| The Great Chapter | {{term|Mahāvagga}} | The Pabbajjā Sutta and Padhāna Sutta, on the going forth and the striving |
| The Chapter of Octads | {{term|Aṭṭhakavagga}} | Discourses on letting go of views |
| The Way to the Beyond | {{term|Pārāyanavagga}} | The questions of sixteen brahmin students |

The fourth and fifth chapters are explained in the canonical commentary, the [[Niddesa]].
