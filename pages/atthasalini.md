The **Atthasālinī** ({{lang|Pāli|Atthasālinī}}), "the Expositor", is the commentary to the [[Dhammasaṅgaṇī]], the first book of the [[Abhidhamma Piṭaka]], compiled in Pāli by the Venerable [[Buddhaghosa]].

Its introduction gives the Theravāda account of the origin of the Abhidhamma: that the Blessed One taught it in the Tāvatiṃsa heaven, gave a summary each day to the Venerable [[Sāriputta]], and that Sāriputta passed it on to his pupils. It also explains the matrix of the Abhidhamma and every type of consciousness and mental factor.
