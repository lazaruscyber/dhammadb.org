The **Puggalapaññatti** ({{lang|Pāli|Puggalapaññatti}}), "Designation of Individuals", is the fourth book of the [[Abhidhamma Piṭaka]]. Unlike the other books, which deal with ultimate realities, it deals with persons, which are concepts ({{term|paññatti}}). It describes types of individuals according to their qualities and spiritual attainments, such as the stream-enterer, the one liberated by wisdom and the one liberated both ways.[^pp]

The types are arranged numerically, in chapters of ones, twos, threes and so on up to tens, in the manner of the [[Aṅguttara Nikāya]], and much of its material is shared with that collection.
