**Javana** ({{lang|Pāli|javana}}), "running swiftly", is the stage of impulsion in a cognitive process ([[Vīthi]]). It is the active phase in which consciousness fully experiences the object, and it is in javana that wholesome or unwholesome [[Kamma|kamma]] is made.[^abhs]

## In the process

In a normal sense-door process, the stage of determining is followed by seven moments of javana, all of the same kind. In a mind-door process there are also usually seven. In a process of absorption into [[Jhāna|jhāna]] or the noble path, the impulsions are fewer in the first moments of preparation, and then may continue for a long time in attainment.[^abhs]

## Kinds of javana

| Person | Javana can be |
|---|---|
| Worldling and trainee | Wholesome or unwholesome |
| Arahant | Functional ({{term|kiriya}}) only: neither producing kamma nor its result |

The arahant still experiences objects, but the impulsion is merely functional, since there is no craving or ignorance to make it kamma.

## The results of the seven moments

The commentaries explain that the kamma of the first javana moment, being weakest, ripens in this life if at all; the kamma of the seventh ripens in the next life; and the kamma of the middle five ripens in any subsequent life until the round of rebirth ends.[^abhs]

[^abhs]: Anuruddha, *Abhidhammattha Saṅgaha*. Chapter IV and V.
