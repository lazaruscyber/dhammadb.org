The **Khuddakapāṭha** ({{lang|Pāli|Khuddakapāṭha}}), "the Minor Readings", is the first book of the [[Khuddaka Nikāya]]. It is a short collection of nine texts that serve as a handbook for novices and lay followers, and several of them are among the most frequently chanted texts in the Theravāda world.[^khpa]

## Contents

1. **Going for refuge** ({{term|Saraṇattaya}}): the formula of the [[Tisaraṇa|three refuges]].
2. **The ten training rules** ({{term|Dasasikkhāpada}}): the precepts of the novice.
3. **The thirty-two parts of the body** ({{term|Dvattiṃsākāra}}).
4. **The novice's questions** ({{term|Kumārapañha}}).
5. **The [[Maṅgala Sutta]]**, on the highest blessings.
6. **The [[Ratana Sutta]]**, the discourse on the jewels.
7. **The Tirokuṭṭa Sutta**, on departed beings outside the walls.
8. **The Nidhikaṇḍa Sutta**, on the treasure of merit.
9. **The [[Karaṇīyametta Sutta]]**, on loving-kindness.

[^khpa]: *Paramatthajotikā*, the commentary to the Khuddakapāṭha and Sutta Nipāta.
