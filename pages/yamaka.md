The **Yamaka** ({{lang|Pāli|Yamaka}}), "the Book of Pairs", is the sixth book of the [[Abhidhamma Piṭaka]]. It clarifies the exact range of technical terms by asking questions in pairs, each the converse of the other: for example, "Is all form the form aggregate? Is the form aggregate all form?"[^ya]

## The ten chapters

:::columns 2
1. Roots ({{term|mūla}})
2. Aggregates ([[Khandha]])
3. Bases ([[Āyatana]])
4. Elements ([[Dhātu]])
5. Truths
6. Formations ({{term|saṅkhāra}})
7. Latent tendencies ({{term|anusaya}})
8. Consciousness ([[Citta]])
9. Phenomena ({{term|dhamma}})
10. Faculties ([[Indriya]])
:::
