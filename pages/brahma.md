A **Brahmā** ({{lang|Pāli|brahmā}}) is a being of the fine-material or immaterial realms. Beings are reborn there through the attainment of [[Jhāna|jhāna]]. Brahmās have no sexual distinction and no sense desire, and their life spans are measured in aeons.[^abhs]

## The Brahmā realms

The fine-material sphere has sixteen planes, arranged by the jhāna that produces rebirth there. The five highest, the Pure Abodes, are reached only by non-returners. Above these are the four immaterial planes, where beings have mind without form. See [[Thirty-one planes of existence]].

## Brahmā Sahampati

After the awakening the Blessed One, reflecting that the Dhamma is deep and hard to see, inclined towards not teaching. The Great Brahmā Sahampati appeared, knelt, and requested: "There are beings with little dust in their eyes who are wasting through not hearing the Dhamma. There will be those who will understand." Out of compassion for beings, the Blessed One surveyed the world and agreed to teach.[^mn26]

## Baka the Brahmā

In the Brahmanimantanika Sutta, the Brahmā Baka held the view that his realm was permanent and eternal, and that there was no escape beyond it. The Blessed One showed him that there are realms beyond his knowledge and that he too was subject to change.[^mn49] In the [[Brahmajāla Sutta]], the view that a creator god made the world is traced to a Brahmā who was the first to arise in a new world-cycle and wrongly believed he had created the beings who came after.

## The divine abidings

The four [[Brahmavihāra|divine abidings]] are named after the Brahmās, as the dwelling of the Brahmā realms; developed to jhāna, they lead to rebirth there.
