The **Abhidhammattha Saṅgaha** ({{lang|Pāli|Abhidhammattha Saṅgaha}}), "Compendium of the Meaning of the Abhidhamma", is a concise manual of the [[Abhidhamma Piṭaka]] composed by Ācariya Anuruddha. In a short text it sets out the whole Abhidhamma system, and for centuries it has been the standard introduction to the subject. In Myanmar it is studied by bhikkhus and lay people alike and often learned by heart.

## The nine chapters

| Chapter | Subject |
|---|---|
| I | Consciousness ([[Citta]]): the 89 or 121 types |
| II | Mental factors ([[Cetasika]]): the 52 factors |
| III | Miscellaneous: roots, feelings, functions, doors, objects and bases |
| IV | The cognitive process ([[Vīthi]]) |
| V | Process-freed consciousness: planes of existence, [[Kamma|kamma]], death and [[Paṭisandhi|rebirth]] |
| VI | Matter ([[Rūpa]]) and [[Nibbāna]] |
| VII | Categories: unwholesome, mixed, the requisites of awakening, the whole |
| VIII | Conditionality: [[Paṭicca-samuppāda|dependent origination]] and the [[Paṭṭhāna]] conditions |
| IX | Meditation subjects ([[Kammaṭṭhāna]]) and insight |

The manual begins with the four ultimate realities ({{term|paramattha}}): consciousness, mental factors, matter and Nibbāna.[^abhs]
