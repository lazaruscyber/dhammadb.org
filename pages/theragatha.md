The **Theragāthā** ({{lang|Pāli|Theragāthā}}), "Verses of the Elder Bhikkhus", is the eighth book of the [[Khuddaka Nikāya]]. It preserves the verses of more than two hundred and sixty elder bhikkhus, many of them spoken as an expression of their liberation. The verses are arranged by number, from elders who spoke a single verse to the Venerable Vaṅgīsa, whose verses are the longest group.

Among the elders whose verses are preserved are [[Aññā Koṇḍañña]], [[Sāriputta]], [[Mahāmoggallāna]], [[Mahākassapa]], [[Ānanda]], [[Anuruddha]] and [[Aṅgulimāla]]. The commentary, part of the *Paramatthadīpanī*, tells the life of each elder.
