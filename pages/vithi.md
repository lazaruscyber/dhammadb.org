A **vīthi** ({{lang|Pāli|vīthi}}), "street" or course, is a cognitive process: a series of distinct moments of [[Citta|consciousness]], each with its own function, through which an object is cognised. Between processes consciousness lapses back into the life-continuum ([[Bhavaṅga]]).[^abhs]

## The eye-door process

The [[Abhidhammattha Saṅgaha]] describes a process with a very great object in seventeen mind-moments:[^abhs]

| Moment | Stage |
|---|---|
| 1 | Past bhavaṅga |
| 2 | Vibrational bhavaṅga |
| 3 | Arrest bhavaṅga |
| 4 | Five-door adverting |
| 5 | Eye-consciousness |
| 6 | Receiving |
| 7 | Investigating |
| 8 | Determining |
| 9 to 15 | Seven moments of [[Javana|impulsion]] |
| 16 to 17 | Two moments of registration |

The life span of a material phenomenon is seventeen mind-moments, so the visible object that arose with the first moment ceases at the end of the process.

## Kinds of process

Processes are classified by the door (the five sense doors or the mind door) and by the intensity of the object: very great, great, slight or very slight. Mind-door processes also include those of absorption into [[Jhāna|jhāna]] and of the noble path, as well as the process at death.[^abhs]

## Speed

The commentaries say that in the time of a lightning flash or the blink of an eye, many billions of mind-moments arise and pass away. Insight meditation discerns this rapid arising and passing in the knowledge of rise and fall ([[Vipassanā ñāṇa]]).

[^abhs]: Anuruddha, *Abhidhammattha Saṅgaha*. Chapter IV.
