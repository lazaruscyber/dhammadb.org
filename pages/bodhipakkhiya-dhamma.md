The **bodhipakkhiyā dhammā** ({{lang|Pāli|bodhipakkhiyā dhammā}}), "the states pertaining to awakening", are thirty-seven qualities in seven groups. Before the parinibbāna the Blessed One gathered the bhikkhus at Vesālī and named these as the teachings that should be learned, practised and developed so that the holy life might last long.[^dn16]

## Fourteen states

Though they number thirty-seven, the [[Abhidhammattha Saṅgaha]] notes that they consist of only fourteen distinct mental factors. Energy appears nine times, mindfulness eight times, concentration four times, wisdom five times, and faith twice; the rest appear once each.[^abhs]

## Ledi Sayadaw

The [[Ledi Sayadaw]] wrote a manual on the thirty-seven requisites, the Bodhipakkhiya Dīpanī, in which the Sayadaw urged lay people to practise them in the present dispensation.

[^dn16]: Dīgha Nikāya 16, *Mahāparinibbāna Sutta*.
[^abhs]: Anuruddha, *Abhidhammattha Saṅgaha*. Chapter VII.
