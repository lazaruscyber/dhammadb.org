The **Veḷuvana** ({{lang|Pāli|Veḷuvana}}), "the Bamboo Grove", in full Veḷuvana Kalandakanivāpa, "the squirrels' feeding ground", was a park outside [[Rājagaha]]. It was the first park given to the Blessed One and the Saṅgha.[^vin]

## The gift

When the Blessed One first came to Rājagaha after the awakening, King [[Bimbisāra]] heard the Dhamma and attained stream-entry. He reflected that the Bamboo Grove was neither too far from the town nor too near, easy to reach, quiet by day and night, and suitable for seclusion. After offering a meal, he took a golden water jug and poured water over the Blessed One's hand, saying "I give this Bamboo Grove to the Saṅgha with the Buddha at its head." The Blessed One accepted and allowed the bhikkhus to accept parks.[^vin]

## Events

At the Bamboo Grove, [[Sāriputta]] and [[Mahāmoggallāna]] were ordained and appointed chief disciples. The commentaries relate that on the full moon of [[Māgha]], 1,250 arahants assembled there spontaneously, all ordained by the Blessed One with the words "Come, bhikkhu", and the Blessed One recited the Ovāda Pātimokkha. This is commemorated on Māgha Pūjā.[^dna]

## The name

The commentaries explain the name "squirrels' feeding ground" by a story: once a king was saved from a snake by squirrels who woke him, and in gratitude he had food scattered there for them.[^dna]
