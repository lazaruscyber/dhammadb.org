The **Paṭṭhāna** ({{lang|Pāli|Paṭṭhāna}}), "Conditional Relations", is the seventh and largest book of the [[Abhidhamma Piṭaka]], also called the Great Treatise ({{term|Mahāpakaraṇa}}). Where the [[Dhammasaṅgaṇī]] analyses phenomena into their elements, the Paṭṭhāna shows how those elements are related to one another by twenty-four kinds of condition ({{term|paccaya}}).[^pt]

The commentaries relate that in the fourth week after the awakening, while the Blessed One contemplated the Abhidhamma in the Jewelled House, rays of six colours shone forth from the Blessed One's body only when the Paṭṭhāna was reached, for there the Omniscient Knowledge found full scope.[^as]

12. Repetition ({{term|āsevana}})
13. Kamma ({{term|kamma}})
14. Result ({{term|vipāka}})
15. Nutriment ({{term|āhāra}})
16. Faculty ({{term|indriya}})

24. Non-disappearance ({{term|avigata}})

## Recitation

The Paṭṭhāna is held in especially high esteem in Myanmar, where its recitation is a regular devotional practice.
