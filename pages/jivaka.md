**Jīvaka Komārabhacca** ({{lang|Pāli|Jīvaka Komārabhacca}}) was the royal physician of King [[Bimbisāra]] and the physician of the Blessed One and the Saṅgha. He was declared foremost among the lay followers who are devoted to persons.[^an1]

## Early life

The Vinaya relates that he was abandoned as an infant on a rubbish heap and found by Prince Abhaya, who raised him; hence "Jīvaka", "he lives", and "Komārabhacca", "raised by the prince". He studied medicine for seven years at Takkasilā. When he asked his teacher when his training would be complete, he was told to search for a league around for any plant that was not medicinal. He returned having found none, and was told his training was complete.[^vin]

## Physician

He treated King Bimbisāra and many others, including a merchant whose skull he opened to remove worms. He treated the Blessed One for a disorder of the humours with lotuses infused with medicine, and dressed the Blessed One's foot when it was injured by a splinter of the rock thrown by [[Devadatta]].[^vin]

## The mango grove

Wishing to visit the Blessed One more often, he gave his mango grove at Rājagaha as a monastery. It was there that King Ajātasattu came to hear the [[Sāmaññaphala Sutta]].[^dn2]

## Vinaya rules

At his suggestion, the Blessed One allowed bhikkhus to accept robes offered by householders, in addition to rag robes. He also urged that bhikkhus who were ill be treated, and asked that those with certain diseases not be ordained, since they sought ordination only to receive his care.[^vin]

[^vin]: Vinaya Piṭaka, Mahāvagga 8.1.
