The Venerable **Puṇṇa Mantāniputta** ({{lang|Pāli|Puṇṇa Mantāniputta}}) was declared foremost among the bhikkhus who teach the Dhamma.[^an1] His mother Mantānī was the sister of the Venerable [[Aññā Koṇḍañña]], who ordained him.

## Teacher

The bhikkhus of his home region praised Puṇṇa as one who had few wishes, was content, secluded and energetic, and who taught these qualities to others. He had five hundred pupils, all of whom kept the ten topics of discussion ({{term|kathāvatthu}}) that he himself practised.[^mn24] The Venerable [[Ānanda]] said that he attained stream-entry on hearing Puṇṇa's teaching on the aggregates and the conceit "I am".[^sn2283]

## The Relay Chariots

The [[Sāriputta|Venerable Sāriputta]], having heard of Puṇṇa's reputation, sought him out at Sāvatthī without telling him who he was. Their conversation is the [[Rathavinīta Sutta]], in which Puṇṇa explains the seven purifications through the simile of the relay chariots. Only at the end did each learn the other's name, and each rejoiced in the other's words.[^mn24]

[^an1]: Aṅguttara Nikāya 1, *Etadaggavagga* (the foremost disciples).
[^mn24]: Majjhima Nikāya 24, *Rathavinīta Sutta*.
