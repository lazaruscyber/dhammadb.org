**Paṭicca-samuppāda** ({{lang|Pāli|paṭicca-samuppāda}}), "dependent origination", is the teaching of how suffering and the round of rebirth arise through conditions and how they cease. On the night of the awakening the Blessed One reflected on it in forward and reverse order.[^ud1]

## The principle

:::quote Saṃyutta Nikāya 12.21
When this exists, that comes to be; with the arising of this, that arises. When this does not exist, that does not come to be; with the cessation of this, that ceases.
:::

## The twelve links

| No. | Link | Pāli |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Ignorance | {{term|avijjā}} |
| 2 | Volitional formations | {{term|saṅkhārā}} |
| 3 | Consciousness | {{term|viññāṇa}} |
| 4 | Mentality-materiality | {{term|nāmarūpa}} |
| 5 | The six sense bases | {{term|saḷāyatana}} |
| 6 | Contact | {{term|phassa}} |
| 7 | Feeling | {{term|vedanā}} |
| 8 | Craving | {{term|taṇhā}} |
| 9 | Clinging | {{term|upādāna}} |
| 10 | Existence | {{term|bhava}} |
| 11 | Birth | {{term|jāti}} |
| 12 | Ageing and death | {{term|jarāmaraṇa}} |

With ignorance as condition, formations arise; with formations as condition, consciousness; and so on, until birth gives rise to ageing and death, sorrow, lamentation, pain, grief and despair. "Such is the origin of this whole mass of suffering." With the remainderless fading away and cessation of ignorance comes the cessation of formations, and so on to the cessation of the whole mass of suffering.[^sn121]

## Three lives

The [[Visuddhimagga]] and the [[Abhidhammattha Saṅgaha]] explain the twelve links across three lives: ignorance and formations belong to the past; consciousness through existence to the present; birth and ageing-and-death to the future. They also arrange the links into four groups, twenty modes and three connections, and describe the three rounds of defilement, kamma and result.[^vism]

## Its depth

When the Venerable [[Ānanda]] said that dependent origination seemed clear to him, the Blessed One replied that it is deep and appears deep, and that through not understanding it beings are entangled like a matted ball of thread.[^dn15]
