The Venerable **Rāhula** ({{lang|Pāli|Rāhula}}) was the son of the Bodhisatta and [[Yasodharā]], born on the day the Bodhisatta went forth. He was declared foremost among the bhikkhus who are eager for training.[^an1]

## The inheritance

When the Blessed One first returned to [[Kapilavatthu]], Yasodharā sent the seven-year-old Rāhula to ask for his inheritance. Rāhula followed the Blessed One, saying "Give me my inheritance, recluse." The Blessed One reflected that worldly wealth brings suffering, and had him go forth as a novice under the Venerable [[Sāriputta]]: the first [[Sāmaṇera|novice]]. King [[Suddhodana]], grieved, asked that in future no child be ordained without parents' permission, and the Blessed One made this a rule.[^vin]

## Instructions to Rāhula

Several suttas record the Blessed One's teachings to Rāhula at different ages:

| Sutta | Teaching |
|---|---|
| Ambalaṭṭhikārāhulovāda Sutta (MN 61) | At age seven: never to lie, even in jest; to reflect before, during and after every action, like looking in a mirror |
| Mahārāhulovāda Sutta (MN 62) | At age eighteen: the elements, meditation like earth, water, fire, air and space, and mindfulness of breathing |
| Cūḷarāhulovāda Sutta (MN 147) | At the higher ordination: the six bases as impermanent and non-self |

On hearing the last, Rāhula attained arahantship.[^mn147]

## Eagerness

The commentary says that each morning Rāhula would take a handful of sand and wish: "May I receive as much instruction today from the Blessed One and my teachers as there are grains here."[^mp]
