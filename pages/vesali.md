**Vesālī** ({{lang|Pāli|Vesālī}}) was the capital of the Licchavis, the leading clan of the Vajjian confederacy, which was governed by an assembly. The Blessed One often stayed at the Kūṭāgārasālā, the hall with the peaked roof, in the Great Wood ({{term|Mahāvana}}).[^dn16]

## The Ratana Sutta

When Vesālī was struck by famine, disease and fear of non-human beings, the Licchavis invited the Blessed One from [[Rājagaha]]. As the Blessed One arrived, heavy rain fell, and the [[Ratana Sutta]] was recited around the city.[^khpa]

## The order of nuns

At Vesālī, [[Mahāpajāpatī Gotamī]] and the Sakyan women came to ask again for the going forth, and the order of [[Bhikkhunī|nuns]] was founded.[^vin]

## The last year

The [[Mahāparinibbāna Sutta]] records the Blessed One's last stays at Vesālī: the courtesan Ambapālī offering her mango grove; the last rains at Beluva, where the Blessed One fell gravely ill; and at the Cāpāla shrine, the relinquishing of the life process, three months before the parinibbāna. On leaving, the Blessed One turned to look back at Vesālī with the elephant's look and told Ānanda it was the last time.[^dn16]

## The seven conditions of welfare

Before the last journey, the Blessed One told the minister Vassakāra that the Vajjians would not decline as long as they kept seven conditions, such as meeting often and in harmony, and honouring their elders and shrines. The Blessed One then taught seven corresponding conditions for the Saṅgha.[^dn16]

## The Second Council

A hundred years after the parinibbāna, the [[Second Council]] was held at the Vālikārāma in Vesālī.
